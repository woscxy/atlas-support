Filter rules and licenses

The rules Atlas ships

Atlas converts community filter lists into Safari's content blocker format. The converted rules bundled with the app are published here under the same licenses as the lists they come from. On your device, the app downloads newer lists straight from their maintainers and converts them there.

Converted rules (bundled with the app)

  • ads.json: converted from EasyList (CC BY-SA 3.0, dual-licensed with GPL-3.0).
  • privacy.json: converted from EasyPrivacy (CC BY-SA 3.0, dual-licensed with GPL-3.0).
  • annoyances.json: converted from the EasyList Cookie List (CC BY 3.0).

Each file also contains the exception rules of the other lists, which Safari needs in every blocker. The converted rules are licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0; attribution: the EasyList authors (easylist.to).

Lists the app can use

The app lists every filter list it uses, with its authors, license and source, under About & privacy. Regional lists are converted on your device when they apply to your region and languages.
